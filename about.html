Our Story

Creativity, confidence, and family are at the heart of GLAM & CRAFT CO. Every set is a chance to show a little more of who you are.

How It All Started

My love for nails, crafts, and creativity started when I was very young. Doing my own nails was something I genuinely enjoyed, and as people began noticing my designs and asking me to do their nails, I started to see a possibility: maybe a hobby I loved could grow into something of my own.

Around the ages of 10–12, the idea of starting a press-on nail business began to take shape. I discovered how much I enjoyed creating sets and bringing ideas to life. That little idea became the beginning of GLAM & CRAFT CO.

I'm also inspired by my mom, who is a lawyer and leads her own company. Watching her work with confidence, organization, and attention to detail encouraged me to build something of my own. I wanted my business to bring together creativity and professionalism.

More Than Just Nails

To me, nails are wearable art. A design can celebrate a holiday, match a mood, show off a favorite color, or add a little sparkle to an ordinary day. I love that you can carry your creativity with you and let your hands make a statement.

My hope is that every customer feels extra, confident, and special when wearing their nails. Whether you choose a ready-to-love design or dream up something custom, I want your set to feel like you.

The Meaning Behind the Brand

Every part of our name has a little meaning behind it:

GLAM

The sparkle, confidence, and special feeling that comes with expressing your own style.

& CRAFT

The creativity, care, and hands-on artistry that go into dreaming up each nail design.

CO.

Community, family, and the people who make this journey meaningful. It's a reminder that this brand is built with love and support.

Family, Pride & Big Dreams

GLAM & CRAFT CO. is a Black family-run business built around creativity, ambition, and family pride. One of my biggest goals is to keep learning, grow this business, make my family proud, and show that a small idea can become something meaningful with care and determination.

Thank you for supporting this creative journey and letting GLAM & CRAFT CO. add a little extra glam to your life. ✧
