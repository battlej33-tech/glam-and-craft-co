Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you place a request.

How do I request a nail design?

Visit Custom Orders and complete the request form. The form needs a connected business email or trusted form service before it can send requests.

How long will an order take?

GLAM & CRAFT CO. is student-run, so school responsibilities come first. Timing can vary, and we'll do our best to respond as promptly as possible.

Can I customize a design?

Yes. Add your preferred colors, shape, length, and special requests to the custom order form.

Where can I see prices?

Each design page will show its own confirmed price once the catalog is finalized.
